## 2. 信息选取与比较评析

### 先分清纵轴

纵轴是**价格相对上个月的百分比变化**，不是金属价格，也不是相对一月的累计变化。

- 镍1月为+6%，2月为+4%：价格仍上涨，但涨幅缩小。不能说“价格从6%跌到4%”。
- 镍6月为−3%，7月为−1%：变化率回升，但价格仍比上个月低1%。不能据此说7月价格反弹。
- 铜7–9月均为+1%：价格每月上涨1%，不是价格水平保持不变。

你在P3-S3写“价格上涨、涨幅越来越低”，方向基本正确；P4-S1写铜下半年持续上涨，也符合图中各月变化率为正。这两处不能误判为完全混淆图意。需要修正的是概述中“价格稳定”的范围，以及变化率作主语时的表达精度。

### 原稿已选对什么

1. 镍是波动最大的系列：月度变化率从+6%降到−3%，范围9个百分点。
2. 铜变化率最稳定：约−0.5%至+2%，范围明显小于镍和锌。
3. 6月的三项读数及7–10月镍跌幅大于锌的比较都可保留。
4. 按上半年、下半年分组合理，不必为了“高级”强行改成逐金属分段。

## 3. 全文逐句批改

“最小修改”优先保留你的信息与句式；必要的数据纠正会明确说明。可接受表达不会仅因不够高级而判错。

### P1 引言

**P1-S1 原句**
> The line chart gives information about how average monthly prices of copper, nickel and zinc changed compared to the last month in 2014.

【词汇/搭配】【逻辑/切题】the last month容易被理解成“最后一个月”，应为the previous month。“average monthly prices…changed”能够传达比较意图，但还应点明百分比变化，避免读者以为图上直接给出价格。

**最小修改：** The line chart gives information about the average monthly percentage changes in the prices of copper, nickel and zinc in 2014, compared with the previous month.

### P2 概述

**P2-S1 原句**
> It is obvious that all kinds of metals experienced both increase and decrease in terms of prices.

【语法】此处用复数increases and decreases更自然，或用both an increase and a decrease。【逻辑/切题】all kinds of metals扩大了图的范围，应为all three metals。【可选润色】It is obvious that不算语法错误，但Overall更直接；in terms of prices可简化为in their prices。

**最小修改：** It is obvious that all three metals experienced both increases and decreases in their prices.

**P2-S2 原句**
> The most significant fluctuation can be observed in the price of nickel.

语法正确，can be observed可接受，不必因图表在过去就强行判错。【逻辑/切题】为准确对应纵轴，将price明确成monthly percentage change；significant在这里可理解为“明显的”，不必牵涉统计显著性。

**最小修改：** The most significant fluctuation can be observed in the monthly percentage change in the price of nickel.

**P2-S3 原句**
> However, the price of copper kept relatively stable in 2014.

【词汇/搭配】描述状态用remained relatively stable比kept relatively stable自然。【逻辑/切题】图直接证明的是铜的月度变化率波动较小，不是给出绝对价格曲线。

**最小修改：** However, the monthly percentage change in the price of copper remained relatively stable in 2014.

**两句升级合并：** Nickel showed the greatest variation in monthly price changes, whereas copper’s figures were comparatively stable.

这里whereas直接建立两种金属的对比；升级不是因为原句使用简单句就有问题。

### P3 上半年

**P3-S1 原句**
> In January, the biggest increase was allocated to Nickle in terms of increasing rate, with the figure being 6%.

【词汇/搭配】allocated to意为“被分配给”，不能表示某金属出现最大涨幅；in terms of increasing rate重复且不自然。【拼写/标点】Nickle → nickel，普通金属名称不需大写。6%正确。

**最小修改：** In January, the biggest price increase was recorded for nickel, with the figure being 6%.

**升级：** Nickel recorded the largest monthly increase in January, at 6%.

recorded不是“分配”，at 6%直接附上幅度，省去冗余结构。

**P3-S2 原句**
> However, the price increasing rates of copper and zinc were closed to each other, with the figures being 2% and 1% respectively.

【词汇/搭配】closed是“关闭的”，表示“接近”用close；price increasing rates改为rates of price increase。【衔接/指代】与镍6%相比，强调铜和锌的涨幅更小，比单说二者接近更有用，但原比较不是数据错误。respectively对应关系正确。

**最小修改：** However, the rates of price increase for copper and zinc were close to each other, with the figures being 2% and 1% respectively.

**可选升级：** The corresponding figures for copper and zinc were much lower, at 2% and 1%, respectively.

**P3-S3 原句**
> From February to May, the price of nickel saw a increase with the rate which was gradully lower, and experienced a decrease in June, when the price was 3% lower than that in May.

【语法】a increase → an increase。【拼写/标点】gradully → gradually。【词汇/搭配】with the rate which was gradually lower生硬，可用at a generally declining rate。【逻辑/切题】镍3、4月涨幅相同，generally避免声称逐月下降；6月较5月低3%正确。and experienced与前面的saw共享主语，结构本身并非残句。

**最小修改：** From February to May, the price of nickel increased at a generally declining rate, and fell in June, when it was 3% lower than in May.

**升级：** Nickel’s monthly growth rate fell from 4% in February to 0.5% in May, before turning negative at −3% in June.

增长率作主语后，fell表达的是涨幅下降；turning negative明确跨过零线。

**P3-S4 原句**
> At the end of the first half year, the prices of copper and zinc were about 0.5% and 1% lower comparing to the previous month.

【词汇/搭配】the first half of the year更自然。【语法】lower comparing to → lower than in；若用compared with也应是过去分词形式。6月这两个近似值正确。

**最小修改：** At the end of the first half of the year, the prices of copper and zinc were about 0.5% and 1% lower than in the previous month, respectively.

### P4 下半年

**P4-S1 原句**
> From July to December, the price of copper kept rising, with the rising rate fluctuating around 1%.

主要意思正确：铜这六个月的变化率均为正，价格确实持续上涨。【词汇/搭配】rising rate应为rate of increase；fluctuating around 1%可接受，图中范围约0.5%–1.5%。kept rising不必强行替换。

**最小修改：** From July to December, the price of copper kept rising, with the rate of increase fluctuating around 1%.

**P4-S2 原句**
> During the first four months of the second half year, there was a decrease in the price of nickel with the rate between 1% and 2%, which was twice higher than that of zinc.

【词汇/搭配】the second half of the year；说each month明确这是每月跌幅，不是四个月累计跌幅。【语法】【衔接/指代】which挂在长串名词后比较对象不够清楚；twice higher含义不稳定，应为twice as large或twice that of，比较对象是跌幅。这里的1%–2%及与锌的两倍关系正确。

**最小修改（拆成两句，避免指代含混）：** During the first four months of the second half of the year, the price of nickel fell by between 1% and 2% each month. Its percentage decline was twice that of zinc.

**P4-S3 原句**
> After November, both nickel and zin recorded an increase in their prices, with the figures being roughly 1% and 1.5% respectively.

【拼写/标点】zin → zinc。【逻辑/切题】after November指向12月，而1.5%是锌11月值。若保留“11月之后”的时间含义，必须把锌改为2%；若本意是11月开始回升，应明确in November。不能只纠正拼写。

**最小修改（按原句时间理解为12月）：** In December, both nickel and zinc recorded increases in their prices, with the figures being roughly 1% and 2%, respectively.

**升级（完整交代转折和终点）：** Both metals returned to positive monthly growth in November; by December, the figures stood at 1% for nickel and 2% for zinc.

## 6. 如何自然带上数据：替代 with the figure being… 和 at

**最值得掌握的是让数字直接进入句子：动词后接幅度、名词后接数值，或把两个对象放在一句中比较。** 不必每次先描述趋势，再接with the figure being…。下面例句均使用本图数据；其中近似读数沿用图中的刻度。

### A. 单个数值：直接写幅度或读数

| 句式                                         | 本图例句                                                                  | 用法                                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **价格 + rose/fell by + 百分比**                | Nickel prices rose **by 6%** in January.                              | by引出价格变化的幅度，最简洁常用。                  |
| **an increase/a fall of + 百分比**            | Nickel recorded **a price increase of 6%** in January.                | 动词后的by换成名词后的of；也可写a decline of 3%。  |
| **There was + a rise/drop of + 百分比 + in…** | There was **a drop of 3% in nickel prices** in June.                  | 用There was引入变化；正确自然，但不必每句都用。        |
| **变化率 + was + 数值**                         | The monthly percentage change in copper prices **was −0.5%** in June. | was完全可以用，数字清楚比刻意换复杂词重要。             |
| **价格 + was + 百分比 + higher/lower than…**    | In June, nickel’s price was **3% lower than in May**.                 | 明确两期价格的相对差异；本图可以比较相邻月，不能任意推断跨月累计涨跌。 |
| **a + 数字% + rise/fall + in…**              | June saw **a 3% fall in nickel prices**.                              | 数字作名词前的修饰语；a 3% fall不再加of。          |

同一个事实可以写成：

- Nickel prices **rose by 6%** in January.
- Nickel recorded **an increase of 6%** in its price in January.
- There was **a 6% increase in nickel prices** in January.

优先用第一种；后两种用于调整句式，不代表分数自动更高。

### B. 一句写两个或三个数：比较通常比孤立报数更有用

| 句式                                       | 本图例句                                                                                                               | 用法                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **…, compared with + 数值 + for…**         | Nickel prices rose by **6%** in January, **compared with 2% for copper and 1% for zinc**.                          | 共用“价格涨幅”的含义，后半句不必重复整套结构。                    |
| **…, while/whereas + 完整分句**              | In June, nickel prices fell by **3%**, **whereas copper prices fell by only 0.5%**.                                | 两个完整分句直接对比；only强调铜的跌幅较小。                    |
| **A and B + 动词 + X and Y, respectively** | Copper and zinc prices rose by **2% and 1%, respectively**, in January.                                            | 顺序必须一一对应：铜→2%，锌→1%。                         |
| **…, with + 对象 + 动词-ing + by…**          | In January, nickel prices rose by **6%**, **with copper and zinc recording increases of 2% and 1%, respectively**. | 保留with结构，但写出具体对象和变化，不再泛泛说the figures being。 |
| **数值 + compared with + 数值**              | The January increase for nickel was **6%, compared with 2% for copper**.                                           | 简单系表结构也能清楚完成比较，无需硬塞高级动词。                    |

**注意省略后的含义必须一致。** “Nickel prices rose by 6%, compared with 2% for copper”中的2%同样指价格涨幅；不要把价格、增长率和所占比例混在同一句省略结构里。

### C. 一段时间：起点、终点、范围和极值

| 句式 | 本图例句 | 用法 |
|---|---|---|
| **变化率 + fell/rose from X to Y** | Nickel’s monthly growth rate fell **from 4% in February to 0.5% in May**. | from为起点、to为终点；这里主语必须是增长率，而非价格。 |
| **变化率 + reached + 数值** | Zinc’s monthly price increase **reached 3%** in February. | reach直接接数字，不加to或at。 |
| **变化率 + reached a peak/low of + 数值** | Nickel’s monthly percentage change **reached a low of −3%** in June. | a low of直接接最低读数；负值在这里保留负号。 |
| **变化率 + ranged from X to Y** | Copper’s monthly percentage changes **ranged from −0.5% to 2%** during the year. | 描述整个时期的最小值至最大值，不暗示按这个顺序变化。 |
| **变化率 + fluctuated between X and Y** | Copper’s monthly growth rate **fluctuated between 0.5% and 1.5%** in the second half of the year. | 强调区间内有变化；between搭配and，不能写between…to…。 |
| **变化率 + remained unchanged + 时间；另句给值** | Copper’s monthly growth rate **was 1% in July and remained unchanged until September**. | 直接给起始读数，再写稳定阶段，避免把价格说成不变。 |

**from…to…和ranged from…to…不是一回事：** 前者常叙述时间上的变化，后者报告数值范围。镍全年的读数范围是−3%到6%，但不能因此说它“从年初−3%升到年末6%”。

### D. 差多少、几倍：用比较关系带出数据

| 句式 | 本图例句 | 用法 |
|---|---|---|
| **X percentage points higher/lower than…** | In January, nickel’s monthly growth rate was **4 percentage points higher than copper’s**. | 6%−2%=4个百分点；不要写4% higher来表达这个差。 |
| **rose/fell by X percentage points** | Nickel’s monthly percentage change rose **by 2 percentage points**, from −3% in June to −1% in July. | 变化率上升2个百分点，但7月价格仍下跌1%。 |
| **twice/three times as large as…** | In January, nickel’s percentage price increase was **three times as large as copper’s**. | 比较6%与2%的涨幅，不是说镍的价格是铜的三倍。 |
| **twice that of…** | In July, nickel’s percentage price decline was **twice that of zinc**. | 比较跌幅大小：1%与0.5%；避免twice higher。 |

### E. 本图最容易写错的介词和单位

| 表达 | 含义 | 本图中的判断 |
|---|---|---|
| Prices **rose by 6%**. | 价格上涨了6%。 | 正确描述镍1月价格变化。 |
| Prices **rose to 6%**. | 把价格终值写成6%。 | 不适用于本图；价格绝对值不是百分比。 |
| The growth rate **fell to 0.5%**. | 增长率降到0.5%。 | to引出终值。 |
| The growth rate **fell by 3.5 percentage points**. | 增长率下降3.5个百分点。 | 可描述镍从2月4%降至5月0.5%。 |
| **a fall of 3%** | 跌幅为3%。 | 用正数表达跌幅。 |
| **a percentage change of −3%** | 带正负号的变化率为−3%。 | 也正确；不要写a fall of −3%造成双重负向含义。 |

另外，**accounted for / made up / represented**通常用于“占整体多少比例”，不能当作所有百分比数据的通用替换。本图6%是涨幅，不是镍在三种金属中的份额，因此不能写Nickel accounted for 6%。

### F. 你这篇最值得直接替换的三句

1. **1月三种金属比较：** Nickel prices rose by 6% in January, compared with increases of 2% for copper and 1% for zinc.
2. **6月跌幅比较：** June saw a 3% fall in nickel prices, whereas copper and zinc recorded smaller declines of 0.5% and 1%, respectively.
3. **年末终点比较：** In December, zinc recorded the largest monthly price increase of the three metals, with its price rising by 2%, while copper and nickel prices rose by 1.5% and 1%, respectively.

日常练习先掌握 **rose/fell by、an increase/decline of、from…to…、compared with、while/whereas、respectively** 这六组。它们已经足够写出自然、多样的Task 1；选择句式时先决定要表达“幅度、终值、范围还是比较”，再接数据。
