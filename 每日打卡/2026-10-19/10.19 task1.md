The line chart gives information about how average monthly prices of copper, nickel and zinc changed compared to the last month in 2014.
It is obvious that all kinds of metals experienced both increase and decrease in terms of prices. The most significant fluctuation can be observed in the price of nickel. However, the price of copper kept relatively stable in 2014.
In January, the biggest increase was allocated to Nickle in terms of increasing rate, with the figure being 6%. However, the price increasing rates of copper and zinc were closed to each other, with the figures being 2% and 1% respectively. From February to May, the price of nickel saw a increase with the rate which was gradully lower, and experienced a decrease in June, when the price was 3% lower than that in May. At the end of the first half year, the prices of copper and zinc were about 0.5% and 1% lower comparing to the previous month.
From July to December, the price of copper kept rising, with the rising rate fluctuating around 1%. During the first four months of the second half year, there was a decrease in the price of nickel with the rate between 1% and 2%, which was twice higher than that of zinc. After November, both nickel and zin recorded an increase in their prices, with the figures being roughly 1% and 1.5% respectively.
