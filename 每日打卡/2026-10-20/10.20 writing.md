## 逐句批改

原稿与提纲保留在上方。以下按两个自然段编号；先给必要修改，再区分可选润色与论证修正。

### 第一段：道路建设

**P1-S1 原句**
> On the one hand, the construction of new roads and road expansion make it more convenient for citizens to drive and stimulates the economic development.

【语法】并列主语对应 make 和 stimulate；泛指经济发展用 economic development，不加 the。【逻辑/切题】增加 can，避免将效果写成必然。

**最小修改：** On the one hand, the construction of new roads and road expansion can make it more convenient for citizens to drive and stimulate economic development.

**P1-S2 原句**
> During time of traffic peak, wider and a larger amount of roads can effectively mitigate traffic jam, significantly shortening commute time of citizens.

【词汇/搭配】高峰期用 during peak hours。【语法/搭配】roads 可数，此处直接用 more and wider roads；整体拥堵用不可数的 traffic congestion。【可选润色】citizens’ commuting times 比 commute time of citizens 自然。

**最小修改：** During peak hours, more and wider roads can help ease traffic congestion, shortening citizens’ commuting times.

**P1-S3 原句**
> What's more, at the societal level, the economy of the society can also be pushed by such convenient transportation.

【词汇/搭配】the economy can be pushed 不自然，改为 stimulate economic activity。【可选润色】at the societal level 和 the economy of the society 重复且空泛。【逻辑/切题】明确是道路连接改善。

**最小修改：** What's more, better road connections can also stimulate economic activity.

**P1-S4 原句**
> For example, cargos can be transported in a comparatively shorter time to destinations, which might contribute to a larger amount of transactions.

【词汇/搭配】泛指货物用 goods 更自然；cargo 也常作不可数名词。【语法】transactions 可数，用 number。【逻辑/切题】might 已有适当限定，但“运输更快→交易更多”仍缺中间机制。

**最小语言修改：** For example, goods can reach their destinations more quickly, which might contribute to a larger number of transactions.

**论证升级：** For example, faster deliveries can reduce transport costs and allow businesses to serve a wider market.

升级补上成本和市场范围，使经济收益有具体依据。

**P1-S5 原句**
> Tourism also gains benifits, with more new roads to landscapes built.

【拼写】benifits → benefits。【词汇/搭配】tourism benefits 更自然；landscapes 指景观，此处旅游目的地应为 tourist attractions。【语法判断】with＋宾语＋过去分词本身可以成立，主要问题是措辞。

**最小修改：** Tourism also benefits when new roads are built to improve access to tourist attractions.

**P1-S6 原句**
> Much more visitors would be willing to come due to convenient transportation.

【语法】visitors 可数，应为 many more visitors。【衔接/指代】come 的目的地不明确。【逻辑/切题】游客大量增加的预测偏强，可用 more visitors may。

**最小修改：** More visitors may be willing to visit these attractions because they are easier to reach.

### 第二段：公共交通

**P2-S1 原句**
> However, optimization of public transport should be prioritzed due to benifits on both transportation and environment.

【拼写】prioritzed → prioritized；benifits → benefits。【词汇/搭配】benefits for，不是 benefits on。【语法】the environment。【可选润色】optimization 并非错误，但 improving public transport 更自然。

**最小修改：** However, improving public transport should be prioritized because of its benefits for both transport and the environment.

**P2-S2 原句**
> Compared to the method mentioned above, improvement in public transport alleviates the traffic pressure relatively more indirectly.

【词汇/搭配】traffic congestion 比 traffic pressure 自然。【可选润色】relatively 和 more 叠加累赘；直接写 road expansion，避免模糊指代。【逻辑/切题】“间接”可以成立，但不能单凭它说明为何应优先投入；后文需要解释如何吸引驾驶者改变出行方式。

**最小修改：** Compared with road expansion, improvements in public transport can ease traffic congestion more indirectly.

**P2-S3 原句**
> Public transport like subways and buses is neither restricted by traffic jam nor expensive to take.

【语法判断】public transport 作单数主语，is 正确；neither…nor 结构也成立。【语法/搭配】用 affected by traffic congestion。【逻辑/切题】普通公交也会堵车，所有公交都便宜也概括过度，需缩小范围。

**必要修改：** Subways are generally unaffected by road congestion, while public transport can be an affordable alternative to driving.

要回应政府“投入”，还应进一步解释投入如何改善服务。

**P2-S4 原句**
> Being chosen by more people, public transport might reduce the amount of private cars on the roads, which would lead to unimpeded roads.

【语法】private cars 可数，用 number。【衔接/表达】Being chosen 不是悬垂结构，但改成条件句更自然。【逻辑/切题】新增乘客未必原本开车，应明确吸引驾驶者转乘；车辆减少不保证道路完全畅通。【词汇/搭配】用 ease congestion 代替 unimpeded roads。

**最小修改：** If more drivers choose public transport, the number of private cars on the roads may fall, helping to ease congestion.

**P2-S5 原句**
> Equally compelling is the benifit for environment.

【拼写】benifit → benefit。【语法】for the environment。【优点】倒装结构正确，可以保留。

**最小修改：** Equally compelling is the benefit for the environment.

**P2-S6 原句**
> Fewer private cars on the road means less significant air pollution.

【语法判断】可理解为“私家车更少这一情况意味着……”，不宜机械地将 means 判成主谓一致错误；改成直接因果结构更稳妥。【词汇/搭配】less significant 容易变成“没那么重要”，用 less air pollution。【逻辑/切题】用 can 限定效果。

**最小修改：** Fewer private cars on the road can mean less air pollution.

**P2-S7 原句**
> What's more, with more electric buses and trams implemented, public transport would be almost zero-emission.

【词汇/搭配】车辆用 introduced 或 put into service；implemented 常搭配政策、措施或系统。【逻辑/切题】增加部分电动车辆不能推出整个公共交通系统几乎零排放；应区分车辆运行时的尾气排放与发电等环节的排放。保留环保观点，但缩小结论范围。

**必要修改：** What's more, introducing more electric buses and trams can further reduce exhaust emissions from urban transport.
