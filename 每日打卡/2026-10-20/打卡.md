### 二、详细逻辑分析

**第一段：道路建设——观点成立，但支持分散。**

原稿逻辑链：新建、拓宽道路→驾车更方便、通勤更快→货物更快到达→交易增加；通往景点的道路增加→游客增加。

“道路改善能促进经济活动”可以成立，问题主要在以下两处：

1. **P1-S4：运输更快，为什么交易就更多？** 这直接证明配送效率提高，尚不能证明需求或交易量增加。应补运输成本降低、配送可靠性提高、企业能够服务更多市场，并将结论限定为支持贸易。

   > Faster and more reliable deliveries can reduce transport costs and help businesses reach more customers, supporting trade.

2. **P1-S5至S6：有新路，游客就会大量增加吗？** 交通便利只是旅游选择的一个因素。若景点原本交通不便，论证更成立；若本来已很方便，再修路未必产生明显变化。

   > Better road access may attract more visitors to tourist destinations that were previously difficult to reach.

改进后的逻辑链：在道路容量不足、连接不畅的地区，新建或拓宽道路→减少延误、改善可达性→降低货运成本并方便游客到访→支持当地经济。

不必删除所有经济观点，但应让货运、旅游共同服务于“改善可达性带来经济价值”，避免列清单。

**第二段：公共交通——已有机制，但缺少有效比较。**

原稿逻辑链：公交不堵且便宜→更多人乘坐→私家车减少→道路畅通→污染减少；增加电动公交和电车→接近零排放。

1. **P2-S3：公交不受堵车影响，范围过大。** 地铁通常独立于地面道路，但普通公交也会堵车。需要解释政府投入如何提高公交吸引力，如增加班次、改善覆盖、设置公交专用道。

   > Investment in more frequent services and dedicated bus lanes can make public transport a more reliable alternative to driving.

2. **P2-S4：车辆减少不能保证道路完全畅通。** 因果方向合理，但结论太满。用ease congestion，不必承诺unimpeded roads。

3. **P2-S2：“更间接”不是应优先的理由。** 比较作用方式尚不能说明哪种方案更值得优先投入。已有的环保论点可以完成比较。

   > Public transport can ease congestion while also reducing reliance on private cars, giving it an environmental advantage over road expansion alone.

改进后的逻辑链：提高公交频率、可靠性与可达性→部分驾驶者愿意转乘→减少私家车出行→缓解拥堵并减少道路交通排放→同时实现交通与环保收益，因此值得优先投入。

### 三、Band 7.5 目标全文提升版

以下补成可独立阅读的完整作文，约280词。保留支持公共交通的立场，以及道路便利出行、促进经济和公交环保的主要观点；补充作用机制、适用条件和优先顺序比较。这些示范补充不计入原稿表现。

Some people argue that governments should prioritise public transport, while others favour building and widening roads. Although better roads can improve accessibility and support economic activity, I believe public transport generally deserves priority in congested urban areas.

On the one hand, road construction and expansion can benefit both drivers and businesses. Where existing roads lack capacity or connections, widening key routes or building new links may reduce delays and shorten commuting times. Better connections can also support the local economy. For example, faster and more reliable deliveries can lower transport costs and help businesses serve a wider market. Similarly, improved road access may attract visitors to tourist destinations that were previously difficult to reach, benefiting local hotels and restaurants. These advantages make road investment particularly valuable in areas with inadequate transport links.

Nevertheless, improving public transport offers a stronger response to urban congestion because it can reduce reliance on private cars. Investment in frequent services, wider coverage and dedicated bus lanes can make public transport a practical alternative to driving. If some commuters switch to these services, fewer cars need to use crowded roads, helping to ease congestion. This shift can also bring environmental benefits by reducing emissions from private vehicles. Introducing electric buses and trams can further reduce exhaust pollution, although the overall environmental impact also depends on how their electricity is generated. Unlike road expansion alone, this approach addresses both traffic demand and air quality, which is why I would give it greater priority.

In conclusion, new and wider roads remain useful where connections are poor. However, governments facing urban congestion should generally prioritise reliable public transport because it can improve mobility while reducing dependence on private cars.

**关键句的论证作用：**

- Where existing roads lack capacity or connections…：限定修路何时更有效。
- faster and more reliable deliveries can lower transport costs…：补全货运与经济收益之间的机制。
- Investment in frequent services…：回应政府投入如何起作用。
- If some commuters switch…：解释公交改善到车辆减少的中间步骤。
- Unlike road expansion alone…：比较方案，直接支持优先顺序。

**独立复核：** 以7–8档为锚点，TR双方展开和优先理由更充分，但示例仍较简洁；CC推进清楚、指代明确；LR搭配准确自然；GRA含条件、让步、定语从句等结构且控制稳定。整体可作为7.5目标示范，不是考试成绩保证，也不表示学生已能独立写出同等水平作文。

**可迁移表达：**

| 表达 | 用途与改善 |
|---|---|
| improve access to… | 表达让某地或服务更容易到达、获得，比roads to landscapes准确。 |
| reduce reliance on private cars | 明确交通政策希望改变的出行习惯，不只是描述车辆减少。 |
| make X a practical alternative to Y | 解释一种方案为什么会被采用，可用于交通、能源、教育。 |
| help businesses serve a wider market | 将经济发展落实为企业能够开展的具体活动。 |
| Where existing… / If some… | 给论点加有效条件，避免绝对化结论。 |

### 四、优先改进清单

1. **每次写“应优先”，补一句比较理由。** 判断标准：读者能否看出它比另一方案多解决了什么问题？
2. **把因果链中的中间行为写出来。** 例如“改善公交→更多人乘坐”之间，补“更可靠、更方便→驾驶者愿意转乘”。
3. **固定检查可数名词与常用搭配。** 重点记：the number of cars / many more visitors / ease congestion / introduce electric buses / benefit the environment。

当前重点是把已有论点写准确、写充分，不是继续增加论点。
