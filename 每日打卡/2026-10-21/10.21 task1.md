## 逐句批改（2026-10-21）

按原稿的五个换行段落编号，共11句；上方原文与附件引用保留不变。数据已与附件三张表核对。综合评分、信息比较分析、全文提升版和训练重点见同目录 [[打卡#写作批改：10.21 Task 1|当日打卡]]。

### P1：引言

**P1-S1 原句**
> The three tables provide information about how population of New York City and its five districts varied during two hundred years since 1800.

- 【语法】特指纽约市人口，应为 `the population of New York City`。
- 【词汇/搭配】`during two hundred years since 1800` 时间表达不自然；已结束的时间段直接用 `between 1800 and 2000`，不是说凡有 since 就一定错。
- 【逻辑/切题】表格将其余四区合并统计，并非分别提供五区数据；此处可理解为全市及其辖区，但最好说明实际分组。
- **最小必要修改：** The three tables provide information about how the population of New York City and its five districts varied between 1800 and 2000.
- 【可选润色】The three tables show changes in the population of New York City between 1800 and 2000, with separate figures for Manhattan and the other four districts combined. 用 `combined` 明确统计口径。

### P2：概述

**P2-S1 原句**
> It is obvious that there was a considerable increase in the population of New York City totally.

- 【词汇/搭配】`totally` 通常表示“完全地”，不能在这里表达“全市总人口”。将“总”放到 `population` 前，用 `total population`。
- `It is obvious that` 语法正确，但没有增加图表信息；换成 `Overall` 只是精简，不算必要纠错。
- **最小必要修改：** It is obvious that there was a considerable increase in the total population of New York City.

**P2-S2 原句**
> A significant decrease can be observed in Manhattan in terms of its proportion of population in New York City, with the corresponding figure rising in other four districts overall.

- 【语法】完整表达用 `its proportion of the population`；特指其余四区用 `the other four districts`，不能漏掉 the。
- 【词汇/搭配】`in Manhattan in terms of…` 绕了一层，`Manhattan's share of the city's population` 更直接；这部分属于表达精确度优化。
- 【逻辑/切题】占比方向正确：76%→54%→19%，其余四区合计24%→46%→81%。但这只概括了比例，未交代曼哈顿人口数量先增后减，详见当日打卡。
- `can be observed` 与 `with + 名词 + -ing` 均可接受，不是语法错误。
- **最小必要修改：** A significant decrease can be observed in Manhattan in terms of its proportion of the population of New York City, with the corresponding figure rising in the other four districts overall.
- 【可选润色】Manhattan's share of the city's population fell, while that of the other four districts combined increased. `that` 明确代替 share，`combined` 避免被理解为四区各自的占比都上涨。

### P3：1800年

**P3-S1 原句**
> In 1800, the total population of New York City was 79,216, with 76% of the population living in Manhattan.

- **正确，可保留。** 总数和占比准确，with 结构也正确。重复 population 可选精简，但不必为避免重复而冒险换词。
- **最小必要修改：** In 1800, the total population of New York City was 79,216, with 76% of the population living in Manhattan.

**P3-S2 原句**
> There were only 18,701 people belonging to the other four districts, accounting for 24% of the total population.

- 【词汇/搭配】`belonging to` 表示归属或成员关系，这里描述居住地应为 `living in`。不是 people 后不能接 belonging，而是本图的含义不匹配。
- 数值与百分比均正确；`accounting for` 表示占总体比例，使用恰当。
- **最小必要修改：** There were only 18,701 people living in the other four districts, accounting for 24% of the total population.

### P4：1900年

**P4-S1 原句**
> The total population saw a huge surge in 1900 compared to the corresponding figure in 1800, with the figure being 3,437,202.

- 数值正确；`population saw a surge` 可接受，`with the figure being…` 也有完整结构，不应判为语法错误。
- 【逻辑/切题】三年快照只能确认1800到1900之间增长，`a huge surge in 1900` 容易让人理解为1900年这一年突然暴涨。用 `had risen … by 1900` 明确终点。
- 【可选润色】figure 重复两次且结构偏重，可把终值直接接在 risen to 后。
- **最小必要修改：** By 1900, the total population had risen dramatically from its 1800 level to 3,437,202.

**P4-S2 原句**
> Although Manhattan had 1,850,093 residents, which was approximately thirty times more than that in 1800, its percentage of total population decreased to 54%.

- 【语法】此处特指全市总人口，用 `the total population`。
- 【衔接/指代】`which` 可指前面的数量信息，但 `that` 的先行词不够直接；用 `the number` 明确比较居民人数。
- 【词汇/搭配】`thirty times more than` 对“多三十倍／为三十倍”有歧义。表中1,850,093÷60,515≈30.57，可准确写为 `approximately thirty times the number`。原句的近似数量级合理，不应判为三十倍完全算错。
- Although 建立“人数增加，但占比下降”的对比是合理的。
- **最小必要修改：** Although Manhattan had 1,850,093 residents, approximately thirty times the number in 1800, its percentage of the total population decreased to 54%.
- 【可选润色】Although Manhattan's population had grown to 1,850,093, its share of the city's total had fallen to 54%. 主句和从句的对比对象更直接，也不必额外计算倍数。

**P4-S3 原句**
> Closely following Manhattan, the other four districts had 46% of the total population.

- 语法成立，46%准确；这里确实是四区合计略低于曼哈顿的54%，不是四个区各自46%。
- 【可选润色】`closely following` 有排名叙述色彩；直接写占比并列比较会更清楚，不能把原句当成硬性语法错误。
- **最小必要修改：** Closely following Manhattan, the other four districts had 46% of the total population.
- **升级表达：** The other four districts combined accounted for the remaining 46%, compared with Manhattan's 54%.

### P5：2000年

**P5-S1 原句**
> The total population of New York City in 2000 was about twice as more as that of 1900, at 8,009,185.

- 【语法】`twice as more as` 混合了倍数等比和比较级结构；用 `twice as large as` 或 `twice that in…`。
- 【逻辑/切题】8,009,185÷3,437,202≈2.33。“约两倍”是较粗略的近似，不是数值颠倒；`more than twice` 更准确且不必计算小数。
- 【词汇/搭配】时间点用 `that in 1900` 更自然，that 指人口数。
- **最小必要修改：** The total population of New York City in 2000 was more than twice that in 1900, at 8,009,185.

**P5-S2 原句**
> However, only 19% of them lived in Manhattan.

- 【衔接/指代】上句说的是单数集合名词 population；`them` 的意思能推断，但没有明确复数先行词。改为 `the city's residents`，避免读者自行补足。
- 【逻辑/切题】19%准确，却不足以说明曼哈顿人数是否下降；需补1,538,096这一终值，详见提升版。
- **最小必要修改：** However, only 19% of the city's residents lived in Manhattan.

**P5-S3 原句**
> The other residents of New York City were allocated to four other districts, which were totally 6,471,089.

- 【词汇/搭配】`were allocated to` 意味着居民被某方分配过去，图表没有提供这种安排；这里只能说 `lived in`。
- 【语法／衔接/指代】`which were` 就近指四个区，却接人口数，造成“区为6,471,089”的对象错误。应用 `a combined population of…` 或让居民作 `numbered…` 的主语。
- 【词汇/搭配】totally 不能代替“合计人数为”；与 P2-S1 属于同类问题。特指剩余四区宜用 `the other four districts`。
- **最小必要修改：** The other residents of New York City lived in the other four districts and numbered 6,471,089 in total.
- **升级表达：** The other four districts had a combined population of 6,471,089, accounting for 81% of the city's total. 同时明确人数和比例；81%来自原图。

### 本次反复出现的模式

- 特指名词短语漏限定：P1-S1、P2-S2、P4-S2。
- 用词未贴合统计含义：totally（P2-S1、P5-S3），belonging to（P3-S2），allocated to（P5-S3）。
- 比较与指代需要明确对象：P4-S2、P5-S1、P5-S2、P5-S3。
